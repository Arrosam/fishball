Why it still reads as two speakers
Round 2 had this the other way round — assistant on glass, user flat. Inverting it costs nothing because the distinction was never which material, only that the two differ.

What it gains: the assistant's turn is the one carrying sources, a meter and a quote card, and a flat opaque plate is a better ground for dense content than a translucent one.

The magenta rule still lands as the user's mark — it is now the only hard edge on that side.

The risk you should judge
Two ambient systems on a three-element screen was the exact thing flagged as “fidgety” in round 1 — and this now has a moving crosshair and a watermark.

They coexist because only one of them moves: the fish is static and sits under everything at 10%, so it reads as printed on the paper rather than as a second animation.

If it still feels busy, the cheap fix is dropping the tick and keeping the two lines.
